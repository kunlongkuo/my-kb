Tools

One default tool for each stage

Script2Film does not synthesize voices, generate video or edit by itself. It splits a film into stages, connects an existing tool to each, and passes work between stages only as files in a folder. That is why any stage can be swapped for the tool you prefer.

Core flow

All of these run on your computer, free

The first example uses only this part. You install the models and packages yourself; the install check tells you what is missing.

Narration

Qwen3-TTS

Local speech synthesis. About ten seconds of reference audio is enough to match your voice, or you can design a synthetic voice from a text description.

Optional

For live footage, a host, generated video of any kind, or a song, consider these options

All of them are optional: you can make a film without them. When you connect one, the tool works inside the account you logged in to yourself: submit, wait, collect, check. The agent asks before spending credits or compute units, and you type your own account name and password.

Generated shots

VideoExpress

Video from text or images. Script2Film works out how long each shot must be, writes the prompts, lays them out as a run sheet, submits and collects them, then measures the clips and checks for bad first and last frames. The project also includes a full set of prompt-writing rules and storyboard tools for VideoExpress.

Runs on
VideoExpress's service
Cost
A one-time-purchase AI video platform with no further fees; output can be used commercially.
Swap for
Any video generator (Seedance, Kling and so on): generate to the run sheet's durations and prompts, then put the files back in the folder under the expected names

Host

TalkingPhotos

One portrait plus text or audio makes a lip-synced host. Script2Film cuts the narration into segments, uploads the look image, submits each segment, collects the results and places them back on the timeline as a circle, a side frame or full frame.

Cost

Generated shots and host

MiniMax H3 (needs a Google Colab account)

An open-weight video model that generates on the Colab GPU of your own Google account. The narration recording drives the host, so the lips follow it, and the prompt can make the person gesture, walk while talking, or film a handheld selfie vlog. It can also generate shots and extend them. Script2Film cuts the narration into parts of 15 s or less, writes the host prompts, checks which country the machine is in before booting, and puts the results back on the timeline.

Runs on
The Colab GPU of your own Google account; your computer needs no graphics card
Cost
Needs Google AI Pro (it includes Colab compute units). Measured on an L4: a 12 s host part takes about 4 minutes; a dozen test clips used about 2 units
License
The open-weight license does not cover the United States, the European Union, the United Kingdom or South Korea; it cannot be used there
Swap for
VideoExpress or TalkingPhotos above, or any tool whose results can be put back under the expected file names
How to connect MiniMax H3
Music and songs

MiniMax Music 3 (needs a Google Colab account)

An open-weight music model. Give it lyrics and a description of the style, and it makes a song with vocals or an instrumental; it can sing in Taiwanese Hokkien and Mandarin. Script2Film writes the job, boots the GPU, generates, downloads and releases the machine, then sets the WAV as the film's background music; you can also paste the code into a Colab notebook yourself.

Runs on
The Colab GPU of your own Google account; your computer needs no graphics card
Cost
Needs Google AI Pro (it includes Colab compute units). Measured on an L4: a 60 s song takes about 5 minutes; booting and one song use about 0.3 to 0.5 units
License
No territory limits; the lyrics must be your own, and published work must be labelled as AI-generated
Swap for
The program-composed music above, or any music you have a license for
How to use MiniMax Music 3
Generated shots

Google Flow (needs a Google AI plan)

Google's video studio, with Omni 1.2 Flash and Veo. A reference image keeps faces and clothes; one continuous action with one camera path flows best, and selfie walk-and-talk vlogs and bullet time work, while large, sweeping martial-arts fights do not yet. Flow has no usable API and Google refuses sign-in in browsers launched by automation tools, so the agent works in your own signed-in Chrome, then files each download with its model, settings and prompt, plus a frame sheet to look at first.

Where it runs
Google's service, through your own Chrome
Cost
Credits from a Google AI Plus, Pro or Ultra plan; the price shows before you send. Measured: 360p, 10 s, two outputs for 20 credits; jobs refused by policy are refunded
Licence
Google does not claim ownership of the output, which carries an invisible SynthID watermark; label a published film as AI-generated
Can be replaced by
VideoExpress or MiniMax H3 above, or any tool whose files you can put back under the expected names
How to use Google Flow
Music and songs

Google Flow Music (needs a Google AI plan)

Google's music studio (Lyria models). Paste lyrics and a style line and a full song comes back in under a minute, downloadable as WAV or stems. It also runs in your signed-in Chrome, and the ingest script sets it as the film's music with its credit in one step.

Where it runs
Google's service, through your own Chrome
Cost
Google AI Pro includes Flow Music's Plus plan, with credits separate from Flow video
Licence
The lyrics must be yours; Google does not claim ownership, but the terms do not say plainly whether commercial use and YouTube monetisation are allowed, so check the current terms before selling or distributing a song
Can be replaced by
The composed music or MiniMax Music 3 above, or any licensed music

Writing and prompts

Prompt Studio

Helps you expand an idea into a storyboard and prompts, for practice or to compare with a storyboard an agent wrote. Its patterns are tuned for VideoExpress. How to split shots, describe characters and write dialogue is worth a look with other AI video tools, but none of it has been tested on any other platform.

Runs on
Your browser (a single HTML file)
Cost
Free, included with the project (MIT)
